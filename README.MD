# 🚀 Space Shooter - Blast Your Way Through the Galaxy! 🌌

Hey there, space pilot! Welcome to **2D Space Shooter**, where your mission is simple: **survive, shoot, and conquer the stars**. Get ready to maneuver through space, take down enemies, and prove that you’re the ultimate cosmic warrior. 🛸💥

## 🎮 What’s This Game About?
Imagine flying through the **depths of space**, dodging incoming threats, and firing lasers at enemy ships. That’s exactly what you’ll experience in this **fast-paced, arcade-style shooter**! 

## ✨ Why You’ll Love It
-  **Epic Space Battles** – Dodge and blast enemies like a true star pilot!  
-  **Simple Yet Addictive Controls** – Arrow Keys to move, Spacebar to fire!  
-  **Immersive Space Backgrounds** – Feel like you’re exploring the universe!  
-  **Explosive Action** – Watch enemies burst into pixels!  

## 🛠 Built With
- **HTML5** (Canvas for space visuals)
- **CSS3** (Styling and aesthetics)
- **JavaScript** (Game logic and controls)

## 🎮 How to Play
1️⃣ Open `index.html` in your browser.  
2️⃣ Use **Arrow Keys** to glide through space.  
3️⃣ Press **Spacebar** to shoot down enemies.  
4️⃣ Keep playing and survive as long as possible!
