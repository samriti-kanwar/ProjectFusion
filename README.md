# 🌟 Project Fusion - Where Shopping Meets Gaming! 🚀  

Welcome to **Project Fusion**, an exciting combination of two unique experiences: **Buy Hive**, an interactive online shopping system, and **Space Shooter**, a thrilling arcade game! Whether you’re looking to shop for your favorite products or blast enemies in space, this project offers **the best of both worlds**. 🛒🎮  

## 💡 What is Project Fusion?  
**Project Fusion** is designed to showcase both **shopping and gaming** within one repository. Here’s what’s included:  
🛍️ **Buy Hive** - A **menu-driven online shopping** system where you can **browse, shop, and manage your cart** effortlessly.  
🚀 **Space Shooter** - A **fast-paced arcade game** where you navigate a spaceship, dodge enemies, and blast obstacles!

## 🛠 Built With  
- **C++ (Buy Hive - Shopping System)** 💻  
- **JavaScript & HTML5 (Space Shooter Game)** 🔥  
- **File Handling & Dynamic Memory Allocation** 🏗️  
- **Canvas Rendering for Space Shooter Visuals** 🌌  

## 🎮 How to Use  
1️⃣ **To shop with Buy Hive:**  
- Run `BuyHive.cpp`.  
- Register/login and start browsing products.  
- Add/remove items from your cart and **place your order**!  

2️⃣ **To play Space Shooter:**  
- Open `index.html` in your browser.  
- Use **Arrow Keys** to move and **Spacebar** to shoot enemies!  
- Stay alive and rack up points!
