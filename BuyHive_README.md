# 🛒 Buy Hive - Your One-Stop Online Shopping Experience!  

Hey there! Welcome to **Buy Hive**, an interactive **menu-driven online shopping** system where you can **browse, shop, and manage your cart** effortlessly. Whether you're looking for **electronics** or stylish **clothing**, we’ve got you covered! 🏪✨  

## 🚀 What is Buy Hive?  
Imagine an online marketplace where you can:  
✅ **Browse available products** (Electronics & Clothing)  
✅ **Add items to your shopping cart**  
✅ **Remove unwanted items** before checkout  
✅ **View your total bill in real-time**  
✅ **Place an order with a shipping address**  
✅ **Register & Login for a personalized experience**  

All handled efficiently using **C++ programming**!  

## 🛠 Built With  
- **C++ (Object-Oriented Programming)** 💻  
- **File Handling (User Authentication & Storage)** 📂  
- **Dynamic Memory Allocation** 🏗️  

## 🎮 How to Use  
1️⃣ Run the program (`BuyHive.cpp`).  
2️⃣ Choose an option from the **menu-driven system** (Register/Login).  
3️⃣ Browse available **Electronics & Clothing** items.  
4️⃣ Add items to your cart and view your total bill.  
5️⃣ Place your order by providing a **shipping address**.
